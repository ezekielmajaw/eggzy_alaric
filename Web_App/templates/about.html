Project Overview

This project focuses on developing an AI-powered Glaucoma Detection system that aids in the early diagnosis of glaucoma, a leading cause of irreversible blindness. The system utilizes advanced image processing and deep learning techniques to provide accurate and efficient results. Our aim is to make glaucoma screening accessible and effortless, ensuring better eye health for everyone.
