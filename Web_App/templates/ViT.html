Model 1 - Vision Transformer

Model Overview

The Vision Transformer (ViT) is a deep learning model that applies transformer architectures, originally designed for natural language processing, to computer vision tasks. Instead of using convolutional layers, ViT splits an image into fixed-size patches, flattens them, and processes them as a sequence using a standard transformer encoder. Each patch is embedded with positional information to retain spatial relationships. Unlike CNNs, ViTs excel at capturing long-range dependencies but require large datasets and significant computational resources to perform well. Variants like DeiT, Swin Transformer, and CvT have been developed to improve efficiency and adaptability, making transformers a powerful alternative to traditional CNNs in visual recognition tasks.

Use Case and Application

The Vision Transformer model is particularly well-suited for identifying and diagnosing glaucoma at an early stage by analyzing high-resolution retinal images. Its ability to capture fine-grained details and spatial dependencies makes it a valuable tool for:

  • Clinical Diagnosis: Supporting ophthalmologists by providing accurate and fast analysis of patient retinal scans.
  • Telemedicine: Enabling remote diagnosis and monitoring for patients in underserved areas.
  • Research: Assisting in studying the progression of glaucoma and its risk factors through automated data analysis.

These applications highlight the potential of ViT in transforming healthcare, improving early detection rates, and making eye care more accessible worldwide.
